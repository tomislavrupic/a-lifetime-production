# A Lifetime Production — microsite design

A public cinematic archive for Tomislav Rupic’s fictional sci-fi horror short and its two making-of mockumentaries. The comedy comes from extravagant production ambition and tiny practical sets. The website presents the films with theatrical seriousness.

## Presentation
Dark charcoal background, cream editorial serif titles, burgundy accents, restrained gold details. Responsive layout; no autoplay audio or distracting animation. Hero: enormous film camera facing a tiny spaceship, serious director. Copy: “A lifetime of preparation. Fifty seconds of cinema.”

## Content
1. Hero with cinematic banner, title and Watch the films button.
2. Screening room: original short, filming BTS, post-production BTS. Native video controls, individual posters, lazy media loading, accessible labels.
3. Cast and crew: original character sheets in a responsive gallery; click to inspect enlarged, Escape closes. Include ALGO only from canonical sheet. Identify fictional characters clearly.
4. Production archive: miniature stage, spaceship, alien instrument, selected approved stills and story notes. Exclude rejected furry takes, personal receipts and private paths.
5. Downloadable landscape banner and social banner, credits and a concise AI-assisted fictional-production disclosure.

## Sources and integrity
Finished exports located under Desktop/Algo scarry story: Algo scary story 2.mp4, Algo scary story 2 BTS.mp4, Algo scary story 3 BTS.mp4. Verify durations and choose correct original before copying. Preserve all source files. Character sheets and stage references come from Back in My Days — Mockumentary. Do not publish generation receipts or paid sound-library source files.

## Delivery
Dependency-free HTML/CSS/JavaScript, relative URLs, GitHub Pages workflow. Repository name a-lifetime-production; check availability first. Optimized web media, images and local poster assets. Large films hosted as repository release assets if unsuitable for Git; do not commit large original videos. No backend, analytics or credentials.

## Acceptance
All three correct films playable; thumbnails and sheets load; keyboard-accessible gallery; mobile layout has no horizontal overflow; no missing local links or private paths. Verify local preview before publication, then Pages deployment and live video URLs separately. Report live URL and commit after verification.
